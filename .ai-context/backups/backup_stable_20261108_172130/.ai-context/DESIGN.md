# Design Architecture: Veritas Forensic — Unified 16:9 Studio Workbench

## Direction & Aesthetic Strategy
- **Structure**: Single, unified 16:9 widescreen studio interface. No disconnected landing/overview screens. The visitor immediately enters the live operational workbench.
- **Palette**: Warm limestone & archival vellum (`#F4F1EA` canvas, `#FAF8F4` working desks, `#EDE6DB` recessed ledgers, `#0F3B5F` Prussian blue anchor, `#B45309` cognac warning, `#047857` verified emerald, `#BE123C` synthetic rose). No eye-straining stark `#FFFFFF` wash, no dark mode.
- **Top Navigation**: Wide floating island capsule (`max-w-[96rem]`) housing pure **Veritas** identity mark, flag visibility toggle, Dossier trigger, reset action, and the primary **Upload** CTA button.
- **Typography & Scale**: Strict adherence to the DESIGN.md typography ramp. Body manuscript reading set to `text-base` (16px) with `leading-[1.8]` at `max-w-[72ch]`.
- **Drop Aperture**: Forensic target ring with continuous rotating dashed orbit ring (`animate-[spin_12s_linear_infinite]`), concentric alignment guide, and deep charcoal core aperture.
- **Telemetry & Decision Column**:
  - Official Adjudication Certificate with `text-6xl` typography, score badge, and empirical conformal quantile readouts (`p < 0.05`).
  - Progressive disclosure diagnostic drawer (`<details>`) grouping the 8 baseline controls.
  - Active safeguards veto strip (LaTeX formula guard, ESL fairness, short-text protection, evasion neutralization).
